# Independent Peer Review

## Purpose

DASS welcomes structured feedback on a research-stage, modular training-device and device-management concept intended only for dedicated inert, non-functional training props.

A peer review on this repository is not formal academic peer review of a published paper, product certification, safety certification or endorsement of real-world use.

## Review scope

Reviews may address:
- Requirements quality and traceability.
- Enclosure concept, material selection, manufacturability and serviceability.
- Electronics packaging, power management and generic device-health monitoring.
- Software architecture, data integrity, offline operation and automated tests.
- Privacy, access control and responsible data retention.
- Verification planning, test reporting and cost analysis.
- Clear separation of synthetic, estimated and measured results.

The repository does not seek feedback on functional-firearm integration, operating mechanisms, aiming or targeting, ballistic or trajectory calculations, recoil replication, or weapon construction, modification or operation.

## How to submit a review

1. Open the [Peer Review issue form](https://github.com/Hatsuneayca/DSS-Shooting-Simulation/issues/new?template=peer-review.yml).
2. Identify the exact commit or document revision reviewed.
3. State the review scope and overall assessment.
4. Separate major findings from minor findings.
5. Cite file paths, requirement IDs, test IDs or public references where possible.
6. State what could not be verified. Do not infer physical performance from synthetic software data.
7. Do not include personal data, credentials, private datasets or sensitive operational information.

## Review handling

Record each substantive finding as:
- **Accepted** — evidence supports the finding and a change is planned.
- **Partially accepted** — the concern is valid but needs a narrower interpretation.
- **Not accepted** — the finding was considered and the reason for not acting is recorded.
- **Unable to verify** — evidence is insufficient.

Changes arising from a review should be linked to the relevant issue and commit.

## Evidence categories

Every result should be labelled as one of:
- **Synthetic:** generated by software for demonstration.
- **Analytical estimate:** calculated from stated assumptions.
- **Bench measurement:** measured under defined laboratory conditions.
- **User evaluation:** collected through an approved usability method.

A software test or successful bench demonstration does not prove field performance, product certification or safety for any use outside the stated inert training-prop scope.

## Reviewer conduct

Be specific, respectful and evidence-led. Disagreement is acceptable; unsupported personal remarks are not.
