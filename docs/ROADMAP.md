# DASS Six-Month Research Roadmap

This is a proposed planning baseline. The supervisor, laboratory availability, procurement lead times and budget may change the sequence.

| Month | Workstream | Main outputs | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Literature, needs and requirements | Comparable-systems matrix, product scope, initial requirements | Supervisor approves scope |
| 2 | Concept and industrial design | Concept selection, CAD enclosure, preliminary material and manufacturing study | Design review |
| 3 | Software and bench demonstrator | Local device-management prototype, electronics bench test, first BOM | Demonstration review |
| 4 | Inert-prop enclosure prototype | Non-functional host integration, serviceability and ergonomic review | Safety/design review |
| 5 | Verification and cost analysis | Software test report, bench measurements, costed BOM, maintenance analysis | Evidence review |
| 6 | Research reporting | Final technical report, limitations, next-phase proposal and presentation | Final academic review |

## Minimum viable demonstrator

Prioritize:
1. One dedicated inert, non-functional training prop as the physical host.
2. A safe, removable electronics enclosure.
3. Device-health and power-status demonstration.
4. Local desktop software with session logging and CSV export.
5. A documented test and cost report.

Defer multi-device fleet management and any optional user-interface notification module until the core demonstrator is stable.
