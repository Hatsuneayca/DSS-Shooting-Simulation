This plan covers software, device-management and inert-prototype validation. It is not a live-fire, weapon-function or recoil-performance test plan.

## 2. Interpretation of scenario analysis

The current `ScenarioMonteCarloEvaluator` repeats a synthetic device-health model using independent random seeds. It demonstrates reproducible uncertainty-analysis and reporting structure.

Its configured dropout probabilities, battery-drain values and temperature changes are illustrative inputs. Resulting availability percentages are not measured failure rates, lifetime predictions, reliability guarantees or evidence of hardware performance. Those claims require calibrated inputs and controlled physical data.

## 3. Inert enclosure and serviceability evaluation

After supervisor and laboratory safety review, the physical demonstrator may be assessed on a dedicated non-functional training prop for:
- Fit and retention of the enclosure on the approved inert host.
- Access to serviceable modules.
- Connector accessibility and strain relief.
- Ease of assembly and disassembly.
- Surface temperature under normal electronic operation.
- General handling durability appropriate to the lab environment.

No test should involve a functional firearm, live ammunition, firing components or a mechanism intended to reproduce weapon operation.

## 4. Electronics and power evaluation

Potential measurements:
- Standby and active current consumption.
- Battery runtime under defined electronic loads.
- Enclosure temperature during a defined bench run.
- Connection continuity and data-loss rate.
- Recovery after software restart or temporary connection loss.

Report test conditions, instrument identification, sample size, raw data and uncertainty where practical.

## 5. Product and maintenance evaluation

Potential metrics:
- Time required to replace a serviceable electronics module.
- Number of unique fastener and connector types.
- Part count and assembly steps.
- Availability of locally sourced components.
- Estimated and supplier-quoted bill of materials cost.
- Maintenance tasks and expected replacement intervals, once supported by evidence.

## 6. Reporting rules

Every result must be labelled as one of:
- **Synthetic:** generated by software for demonstration.
- **Analytical estimate:** calculated from stated assumptions.
- **Bench measurement:** measured under controlled laboratory conditions.
- **User evaluation:** gathered through an approved usability method.

Do not claim field performance, certification, operational readiness or compatibility that has not been independently verified.
