## 2. Cost categories

- Purchased electronic components.
- Enclosure material and manufacturing.
- Assembly and wiring consumables.
- Test equipment (purchase or laboratory access).
- Software and development tools, if not freely available.
- Packaging and transport.
- Contingency, justified by procurement uncertainty.

## 3. Costing rules

- Separate one-time development costs from per-unit recurring costs.
- Separate supplier quotations from estimates.
- Record currency and conversion date.
- State whether tax, shipping and customs are included.
- Identify single-source components and alternatives.
- Do not claim a cost advantage until comparable scope and quantities have been assessed.
